# MusicGenome and flower mapping

`MusicGenome` is the stable boundary between listening mathematics and visual
construction. It is strict, immutable, JSON-serializable, and versioned as
`anthesis-genome-v1`. It deliberately excludes source paths and original audio.

## Contents

- normalized global descriptors for dynamics, rhythm, timbre, harmony, form,
  and expressed affect;
- normalized structural sections with recurrence and contrast;
- a bounded expressive trajectory containing valence, arousal, tension,
  complexity, sublimity, and confidence;
- a robust landmark signature for similarity and exact identity material for
  practical visual uniqueness;
- every analysis-version identifier needed to reproduce the result.

Long trajectories are sampled uniformly to at most 256 points by default.
This changes JSON density only; it does not alter the underlying analysis.
The stable genome digest hashes canonical, key-sorted JSON.

## Botanical mapping

`anthesis-flower-map-v1` converts genome coordinates into a bounded blueprint.
The future renderer will consume this blueprint without reading audio.

- formal complexity and rhythmic density control petal count;
- detected section count controls petal layers;
- sublimity and arousal control petal length;
- harmonicity and warmth control petal width;
- valence and arousal control bloom openness;
- tension controls curvature and accent behavior;
- roughness and formal complexity control edge detail and irregularity;
- pulse clarity and percussiveness control the flower center;
- energy, sublimity, and the expressive arc control the stem;
- key establishes the base hue while valence, arousal, tonal clarity, and
  tension modify pigment character;
- dynamic range and section contrast control painterly stroke variation.

The identity seed contributes only restrained offsets to hue, irregularity,
stem curve, and bloom tilt. It also becomes the seed for the future procedural
geometry engine. Thus musical measurements determine the recognizable
macro-form, while exact song identity prevents two similar songs from
collapsing into the same flower in practice.
